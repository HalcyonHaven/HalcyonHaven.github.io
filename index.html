HAVEN CORE

Halcyon Haven

HAVEN CORE is the private desktop app the Halcyon Haven YouTube channel uses to make its videos. It runs on our own Windows PCs. It isn't sold, and it isn't offered to the public.

What it does

  • Organises our gameplay recordings, bookmarks and notes.
  • Edits and exports videos.
  • Researches which games to play next.
  • Shows how our own videos are doing, so we can plan better ones.

How it uses YouTube

With the channel owner's permission, HAVEN CORE connects to the Halcyon Haven channel through Google's own sign-in, with read-only access. It reads:

  • the channel's name
  • its videos
  • their YouTube Analytics numbers

It can't upload, publish, edit, delete or comment on anything. Everything it reads stays on our own PCs.

The details are in the privacy policy.
