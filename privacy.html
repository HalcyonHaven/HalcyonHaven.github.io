HAVEN CORE privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

HAVEN CORE is the private desktop app of the Halcyon Haven YouTube channel. It runs only on our own Windows PCs. This policy explains what it does with data from Google and YouTube.

What HAVEN CORE accesses

When the channel owner chooses Connect YouTube Account, they sign in on Google's own page. HAVEN CORE never sees or stores their Google password. They approve two read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: the channel's name, subscriber and video counts, and its videos' titles, publish dates and lengths.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: the channel's YouTube Analytics numbers. That's views, minutes watched, subscribers gained and lost, and each video's views, minutes watched and percentage viewed.

HAVEN CORE can't upload, publish, edit, delete, comment on, or change anything on YouTube or in the Google account. It asks for no other Google data.

Separately, HAVEN CORE can use the public YouTube Data API to look up public information about games, such as how many recent public videos a game has.

How it's used

Only to help the channel plan and make videos. HAVEN CORE shows the channel's own performance and the games worth playing next. That data isn't used for advertising, and it isn't used to train AI models.

Where it's kept

  • The sign-in is the token Google issues. It's kept on the PC where the owner connected, encrypted with Windows' own data protection for that Windows user. It's never put in backups, logs or shared folders.
  • The channel's numbers are kept in HAVEN CORE's own database on that PC. They're included in a backup only if the owner saves one themselves.
  • Nothing is sent to any server run by HAVEN CORE or by anyone else. HAVEN CORE has no server.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent, share or transfer data from Google or YouTube to anyone.

HAVEN CORE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • In HAVEN CORE, go to Settings, Integrations, YouTube, and choose Disconnect. This removes the sign-in from the PC and tells Google to end it.
  • Access can also be removed at any time from the Google account, on Google's security settings page.
  • Numbers already read stay on the PC until they're replaced or deleted. HAVEN CORE can be uninstalled at any time, and its data folder deleted.

YouTube and Google

HAVEN CORE uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
